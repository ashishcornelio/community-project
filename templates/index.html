💡 Newton's Third Law of Motion

Newton's third law states: "For every action force, there is an equal and opposite reaction force."

In simple words: When one object pushes another object, the second object pushes right back with the exact same amount of push in the opposite direction!

📌 Everyday Examples:
• Walking: Your foot pushes the floor backward; the floor pushes your foot forward.
• Rocket propulsion: Exhaust gas shoots downward; the rocket is propelled upward into space.
• Recoil of a gun: The bullet shoots forward; the gun pushes backward against the shooter's shoulder.

The French Revolution (1789) - Short Notes

Key Highlights:

• Outbreak: 14 July 1789 in Paris with the historic storming of the Bastille prison fortress.

• Social Structure: French society was divided into Three Estates. The 3rd Estate (peasants, workers, merchants) paid 100% of taxes while 1st & 2nd enjoyed tax-free luxury.

• Important Events: Tennis Court Oath, Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen, Reign of Terror (Robespierre), and rise of Napoleon Bonaparte.

• World Legacy: Universal ideals of Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity (Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité) and abolition of feudalism.
